# Code of Conduct

LinkPi Companion is an open technical project. Participation should remain constructive, respectful and focused on helping people build reliable workflows.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Provide reproducible technical details when disagreeing about behavior.
- Respect privacy: never repost credentials, private logs or configuration data without permission.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about safety and correctness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate credential exposure, spam and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project. Serious concerns should be reported privately through the repository's security/contact channels rather than escalated in public threads.
